---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Definitions of every term used across the commas protocol and documentation."
---

**Token.** The SPL token issued by a market. Its price is tethered to the index by funding and cash-settled shorts. One market has exactly one mint with 6 decimals and a fixed 1,000,000,000 supply, created by the [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) DBC launch with its mint authority revoked. Recorded as `Market.synth_mint`.

**Underlying.** The collectible a market is launched against: a specific graded card (a [PSA](https://www.psacard.com) 10 Umbreon VMAX), a weighted basket of cards, or an NFT collection's floor (Mad Lads). Never SOL, never a stablecoin: SOL is only the currency traded with and the collateral hedgers post.

**Unit of underlying.** What one million tokens represent: one graded card at a specific grade, one basket share, or one floor NFT of a collection.

**TOKENS_PER_NFT.** Protocol constant: 1,000,000 whole tokens represent one unit of the underlying. The name reflects the NFT origin of the design; it applies to every asset class.

**Base units.** The smallest on-chain increment of the token. With 6 decimals, one unit of underlying equals 1,000,000 tokens times 10^6, so 1e12 base units per unit of underlying. All program math uses base units.

**Identity metadata.** The mint's on-chain Metaplex metadata, pointing at commas' `/token-metadata` JSON (name, image, "Underlying" / "Collectible ID" attributes), which makes Solscan and terminals render the token as the collectible.

**Index.** The oracle-reported price of one unit of the underlying, in lamports. Pushed by the oracle authority.

**Index TWAP.** The time-weighted EMA of accepted index observations, computed on chain over `index_window_secs`. Every tether calculation (collateral ratios, funding, liquidations) reads the TWAP, never a raw push.

**Mark price.** The live [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) pool price, in lamports per unit of underlying. commas does not parse the pool on chain; the relayer pushes the mark with `push_mark`.

**Mark EMA.** The time-weighted EMA of the pushed mark, updated on every push and every funding crank over `mark_window_secs`. Funding compares the mark EMA to the index TWAP, so single-transaction pool manipulation cannot move funding.

**Premium.** The signed gap between mark EMA and index TWAP, in basis points of the index. Positive when the token trades rich.

**Funding rate.** The per-day rate derived from the premium (`premium * funding_k_bps`), clamped to `max_funding_bps_per_day` in either direction. When the mark is above the index, funding credits shorts (they are paid); below, it debits shorts (they are charged).

**Funding index.** A global fixed-point accumulator (scale 1e12) that applies funding to all short positions at once. Positions snapshot it at last touch, so no per-position writes are needed when funding accrues.

**Cash-settled short.** A position that posts SOL collateral against a notional valued at the index and settles its PnL in SOL: profit when the collectible falls, loss when it rises. No tokens are drawn or returned. Closed with `close_position`, which settles PnL against the insurance fund. Replaces the retired CDP short.

**Notional.** A short's size, in token base units, valued at the index (`size * index_twap / 1e12`). The collateral ratio and liquidation checks are computed against it.

**Collateral ratio (CR).** Collateral value divided by notional value at the index. Opening or withdrawing requires the initial CR (default 15000 bps, 150%). Falling below the maintenance CR (default 12000 bps, 120%) makes the position liquidatable.

**Open interest cap.** The `max_open_interest` parameter, a cap on total short notional across a market, in base units. No supply is preminted for it; it bounds notional exposure so the synthetic short side never becomes the price setter for its own underlying.

**Program-derived address (PDA).** A Solana account whose address is derived from a program and fixed seeds rather than from a private key. No key for it exists, so only the owning program can sign for it: ***commas***'s vaults, insurance funds, and market accounts are all PDAs, meaning no human holds a key to any of them.

**Relayer (price feed).** The signed off-chain service ***commas*** runs that reads a collectible's real price (live [Magic Eden](https://magiceden.io) floors for NFTs; operator-attested [Collector Crypt](https://collectorcrypt.com) floors for graded cards, converted through [Pyth](https://pyth.network) SOL/USD), and pushes the index and mark on chain with the oracle authority's signature. See [Oracle & Relayer](/oracle/architecture).

**Meteora DBC.** The Dynamic Bonding Curve pool a token launches on. As it fills, Meteora migrates it to a DAMM v2 pool. Trades route through Jupiter and pay a 0.7% base fee. Replaces the retired internal commas bonding curve.

**DAMM v2.** The Meteora constant-product pool a market's DBC migrates into for its live phase.

**Migration.** Meteora's move of the pool from the DBC to DAMM v2, on Meteora's schedule. Not a commas graduation gate: no commas instruction is cranked, and the oracle, funding, and short facilities run across both phases without interruption.

**Item swap.** The value-based exchange between a market's token and registered on-chain copies of its underlying: `deposit_item` places a copy in escrow for `index_twap * 1e12 * units_per_item_micro / mark_ema / 1e6` base units of tokens (less the 0.7% fee), and `withdraw_item` returns the same worth for a copy. Always equal value at execution, net of fee; optional redemption, not custody by default.

**Item reserve.** The token allocation that funds item-swap payouts and receives item-swap repayments (parameter `item_reserve`, PDA seeds `["items", market]`), funded by an SPL transfer at listing since the mint is external and fixed. Zero disables item swaps for the market.

**Item registration.** The admin-created PDA (`["item", market, item_mint]`) marking a specific copy (a vaulted-card NFT or collection NFT) as depositable into a market's item reserve. Registration is admin-gated in v1 while vault provenance is verified off chain.

**Identity escrow.** A keyless system-account PDA (`["escrow", sha256("platform:handle")]`) that accumulates a creator's fee share when a launch names a fee receiver by social handle instead of a wallet. Released to the creator's chosen wallet by the admin-signed `release_escrow` after an off-chain identity proof (X OAuth, YouTube, or Elite Fourum).

**Insurance fund.** Per-market SOL reserve, the bounded counterparty to cash-settled shorts: it pays short profits, absorbs short losses, and covers liquidation shortfalls. Seeded permissionlessly with `fund_insurance` (there is no launch raise to carve it from).

**Circuit breaker.** If an index push deviates from the TWAP by more than `breaker_bps`, the observation is recorded but not absorbed, and the market freezes. Frozen markets block the commas machinery (shorts, closes, funding, item swaps, oracle pushes, liquidations); adding collateral and trading on Meteora stay open. Only the admin unfreezes.

**Oracle authority.** The keypair permitted to push index and mark observations, set in the global config and rotatable by the admin. Held by the price feed service (see **Relayer** above).

**Liquidation bonus.** The premium a liquidator earns over the position's index-valued notional, default 500 bps (5%), paid from the position's collateral and, if needed, the insurance fund. Cash-only.

**Crank.** A permissionless instruction anyone can call to advance protocol state, such as `accrue_funding`. Rate-limited by `min_crank_interval_secs`.

**Unit.** 1,000,000 tokens (1e12 base units), the quote size for the on-chain index and mark. Launch-scaled: set equal to the token's launch price per unit and moving with the collectible from there. `units_per_item_micro` records how many units one physical item was worth at launch.

**Live / Frozen.** Live is the normal market state (the token trading on Meteora with the synthetic layer armed); frozen is a flag that pauses the commas-side machinery.
