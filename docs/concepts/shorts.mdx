The short side of a ***commas*** market is **cash-settled**. A short posts SOL collateral and opens a position of some notional size against the [price index](/concepts/price-index). Its PnL settles in SOL: the short **profits when the collectible falls** and loses when it rises. No tokens are ever drawn, sold, or bought back, and no treasury reserve is touched. The **[insurance fund](/concepts/insurance-fund) is the bounded counterparty**: it pays short profits and absorbs short losses. Total short open interest across a market is capped by `max_open_interest`.

<Note>
This replaces the retired v1 CDP model, where a short drew synth from a treasury reserve at the index, sold it, and later bought it back to `repay_burn`. There is no `repay_burn` any more, and no token reserve: a short is opened against SOL collateral and closed with `close_position`, which settles PnL directly in SOL.
</Note>

## Who shorts, and why

- **Holders of the underlying hedging downside.** A collector holding the physical card or the NFT can open a cash-settled short against SOL collateral, locking in exposure to the current price without selling the asset itself. No sale means no tax event, no loss of the collectible, no exit from the community. This is the structural short supply the market is designed around.
- **Basis traders.** When the mark trades above the index, [funding](/concepts/funding) pays whoever is willing to be short the gap. Traders open shorts to collect that flow and close when the basis normalizes.

## Opening a position

`open_short(collateral, size)` transfers `collateral` lamports into the market vault and records a short of notional `size` (in base units, the token amount the position is short) at the current index. The position must satisfy the initial collateral ratio:

```
notional_value = size * index_twap / 1_000_000_000_000
required       = notional_value * initial_cr_bps / 10_000
collateral >= required
```

With the default `initial_cr_bps = 15000`, a short with a notional worth 10 SOL at the index requires at least 15 SOL of collateral.

Each wallet has exactly one position per market, stored at a program-derived address with seeds `["short", market, owner]`. Calling `open_short` again adds to the existing notional; the combined position must still meet the initial CR.

<Note>
Opens are bounded by the market's open-interest cap: total short notional across all positions cannot exceed `max_open_interest` (`OpenInterestCapReached`), sized against the real depth of the collectible's market. The [insurance fund](/concepts/insurance-fund) is the counterparty that pays profits and absorbs losses, so a market's practical short capacity also tracks the fund's balance. See [Parameters](/concepts/parameters).
</Note>

## PnL

A short's PnL is the change in the notional's index value since entry, settled in SOL:

```
pnl = size * (index_entry - index_twap) / 1_000_000_000_000   (plus accrued funding)
```

The short profits when the index falls below the entry index and loses when it rises. Nothing is realized until close: PnL is a running number against the live index, adjusted by [funding](/concepts/funding).

## Managing a position

| Action | Instruction | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add collateral | `add_collateral(amount)` | Always allowed, even while frozen or stale |
| Withdraw collateral | `withdraw_collateral(amount)` | Post-withdraw CR must still meet `initial_cr_bps`; needs a fresh index while the position is open |
| Close the position | `close_position()` | Settles PnL in SOL against the insurance fund and returns remaining collateral; rent is refunded |

Withdrawals are held to the initial CR, not the maintenance CR, so you cannot withdraw yourself to the edge of [liquidation](/concepts/liquidations).

## Closing: `close_position`

`close_position()` settles the position's PnL directly:

- **In profit** (index fell): the profit is paid to the owner from the [insurance fund](/concepts/insurance-fund), and the full collateral is returned.
- **At a loss** (index rose): the loss is deducted from collateral and paid into the insurance fund; the owner keeps the remaining collateral.

There is no token leg. The account's collateral and notional are zeroed and rent is refunded.

## Funding is applied continuously

Positions do not re-price on every crank. They snapshot the market's global `funding_index` (scale `FUNDING_ONE = 1e12`) at each touch, so a single update to `funding_index` applies funding to every open short at once:

```
// funding accrued to a position is the change in the global index since its last touch
```

When the mark trades above the index, funding credits shorts (they are being paid to hold the tether side); when the mark trades below, funding debits shorts. The credit or debit lands in the position's settleable PnL, realized at `close_position`. See [Funding](/concepts/funding) for the exact rate.

## Risk summary

- If the index rises, your notional value rises and your CR falls. Below `maintenance_cr_bps` your position can be liquidated by anyone. See [Liquidations](/concepts/liquidations).
- Funding can work for or against you depending on where the mark sits relative to the index.
- A [circuit breaker](/concepts/circuit-breaker) freeze pauses closes and liquidations, but you can always add collateral while frozen.
- Opening, withdrawing collateral, closing, and being liquidated all require a fresh index: if the oracle has not pushed within `max_index_age_secs`, these return `IndexStale` until the feed resumes. See the [staleness guard](/concepts/price-index#staleness-guard). Adding collateral still works while stale.
- The insurance fund is a bounded counterparty. If it is exhausted, short profits may not be fully payable until it is replenished; see [Insurance Fund](/concepts/insurance-fund).
