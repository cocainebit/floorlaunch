A ***commas*** token launches on a [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) Dynamic Bonding Curve (DBC) and, as the curve fills, Meteora **migrates the pool to DAMM v2**. This is Meteora's migration, on Meteora's schedule. It replaces the retired v1 "graduation", where the internal commas curve raised a fixed SOL target and the program seeded its own AMM.

## No commas graduation gate

There is no `graduate` instruction to crank and no SOL target the creator or buyers have to cross. commas never holds the curve or the pool, so migration needs no commas transaction: the token keeps trading through the DBC phase, through Meteora's migration, and into the DAMM v2 pool without interruption.

The commas synthetic layer is live from the moment the market is attached with [`create_external_market`](/reference/instructions#create_external_market):

- The market starts directly in `Live` status.
- The oracle **index** and **mark** are pushed by the relayer ([`push_index`](/reference/instructions#push_index), [`push_mark`](/reference/instructions#push_mark)).
- The funding clock, [cash-settled shorts](/concepts/shorts), and [liquidations](/concepts/liquidations) are armed.

## What the DBC to DAMM v2 move changes

For a holder, migration is largely transparent: the token, its metadata, and its oracle market are unchanged, and Jupiter continues to route trades. What changes is the underlying Meteora pool type (bonding curve to constant-product DAMM v2) and its liquidity profile. Because commas reads the pool only through the oracle-pushed [mark](/concepts/amm), the funding and risk machinery is indifferent to which Meteora pool type is live.

<Note>
Migration does not move holders' tokens or reset any commas state. The oracle index, `mark_ema`, and the funding index all carry across. Nothing about a position's collateral, PnL, or liquidation threshold changes at migration.
</Note>

## The insurance fund

In v1 the insurance fund was seeded from a share of the internal curve's raise at graduation. There is no such raise on the Meteora path, so the [insurance fund](/concepts/insurance-fund) is instead seeded permissionlessly through [`fund_insurance`](/concepts/insurance-fund#how-it-is-funded): anyone can deposit SOL to back the short side of a market. The fund is the bounded counterparty for cash-settled shorts, so a market's usable short capacity tracks the fund's balance and the `max_open_interest` cap.

## Design intent

Putting the money-holding curve on Meteora means the highest-risk piece of any launchpad runs on the most battle-tested contracts on Solana rather than on fresh code, and the token is visible on every Solana terminal from launch. commas adds only the synthetic layer on top: the oracle index, the funding tether, the cash-settled short facility, and the item-swap door.
