On every other launchpad, a token is backed by nothing but attention. On ***commas***, every token is launched **against a real collectible market**. The creator picks the underlying: a specific graded card (a [PSA](https://www.psacard.com) 10 Umbreon VMAX, say) or an NFT collection floor (Mad Lads). Buyers get liquid exposure to that collectible's price, and the token tracks it through a live oracle index plus funding. The token launches as a [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) Dynamic Bonding Curve pool and trades like any Solana token, visible on Axiom, Photon, Solscan, and Jupiter. SOL is just the money you pay with; it is never the thing being tracked. One million tokens represent one unit of the collectible: one graded card, or one floor NFT.

## Launching a market means choosing an underlying

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the collectible">
    A market is listed for one underlying: one card at one grade, a basket of cards, or one NFT collection's floor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The market gets a price feed">
    A signed oracle reads the collectible's real price ([Collector Crypt](https://collectorcrypt.com) floors for graded cards, live [Magic Eden](https://magiceden.io) floors for NFT collections), converts to SOL, and writes the result on chain as the market's index.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The token launches as a Meteora DBC pool">
    commas creates a fresh SPL mint (fixed 1,000,000,000 supply, mint authority revoked) and a [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) Dynamic Bonding Curve pool. Anyone can buy from the first block through Jupiter; as the curve fills it migrates to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The token tracks the collectible">
    Funding pulls the token's pool price toward the collectible's oracle index, up and down. Card doubles, your exposure doubles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The protocol custodies nothing to back the token. There is no mandatory vault, no redemption queue, and no pooled collateral priced by an oracle. The token *is* the collectible's price and identity: on-chain [Metaplex metadata](/concepts/synth-token#identity) brands the mint as the collectible so it renders as the underlying on Solscan and terminals, and an on-chain oracle index carries the collectible's price. The tether to that index comes from a **clamped funding rate** and from **cash-settled shorts**, the same shape that pegs a perpetual future, applied to a token. Every token is a fixed-supply SPL mint (1,000,000,000, all tradeable) whose mint authority is revoked at creation. On top of that, markets can run an [item swap](/concepts/synth-token#item-swaps): a holder of the designated collectible swaps the real item for tokens at the oracle value (and back), so the collectible has an on-chain door in and out without being what the token's solvency depends on.

<Note>
***commas*** is live on Solana mainnet. Program ID: `QsixfrupxfVEDDYuQsR4vJcE58bbNfctD9WjijM9BjM`. The Meteora DBC venue replaces the retired internal bonding curve. NFT floors read live from [Magic Eden](https://magiceden.io); graded-card prices are operator-attested, seeded from [Collector Crypt](https://collectorcrypt.com) floors and converted through [Pyth](https://pyth.network) SOL/USD (a fully automated graded feed is planned, not live: pages that touch it say so).
</Note>

## Who it's for

- **Longs.** Anyone who wants price exposure to a PSA 10 card or a blue-chip NFT floor without buying, storing, grading, or insuring the real thing. Buy the token on the Meteora pool through Jupiter; sell it whenever you like.
- **Hedgers (the natural shorts).** Holders of the underlying can post SOL collateral and open a **cash-settled short**: their PnL settles in SOL against the oracle index, so they lock in today's price without selling their card or NFT. They are structurally motivated to supply the short side, which is what most floor-derivative designs lack.
- **Liquidators and crankers.** Funding accrual and liquidations are permissionless. Keepers earn a bonus for closing undercollateralized shorts.

## Why this design

Every earlier attempt at collectible price exposure died in one of two ways: vault models (fractionalization) filled with the worst items and amplified crashes, and oracle-settled perps got drained the first time someone manipulated a thin floor print. ***commas*** is shaped around those failure modes:

- **No vault dependency.** The token's solvency never rests on custodied items, so there is nothing to fill with lemons and no reflexive redemption spiral. The optional [item swap](/concepts/synth-token#item-swaps) accepts only admin-registered copies of the exact card+grade (fungible by construction) and prices every swap at live value, so it cannot be gamed by dumping inferior items at a fixed rate.
- **Bounded oracle damage.** A bad index print cannot unlock anyone's funds. It can only tilt the funding rate, which is clamped per day, and a print that deviates too far from the TWAP trips a circuit breaker that freezes the commas-side machinery.
- **A bounded counterparty.** The short side settles against a per-market [insurance fund](/concepts/insurance-fund), and total short open interest is capped by `max_open_interest`, sized against the real depth of the collectible's market. The synthetic can never grow large enough to become the price setter for its own underlying.
