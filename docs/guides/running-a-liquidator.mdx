---
title: "Running a Liquidator"
description: "Keeper guide: monitor short positions, liquidate underwater ones cash-only, and run the funding crank."
---

Liquidations on ***commas*** are permissionless and **cash-only**. Anyone can
close a short once it falls below maintenance collateral and earn a SOL bonus
for doing so; you need no token inventory and no upfront capital beyond
transaction fees. Keepers also run the permissionless funding crank. This page
covers both jobs.

## Watching positions

Every short is a `ShortPosition` account at a program-derived address
(`["short", market, owner]`). Fetch all of them per market with
`getProgramAccounts` filtered on the account discriminator and the market
field, then compute each position's health:

```
notional_value = size * market.index_twap / 1e12     (lamports)
liquidatable   = collateral * 10000 < notional_value * maintenance_cr_bps
```

`size` is the position's short notional; funding is folded into its settleable
PnL. `funding_index` and `index_twap` both live on the `Market` account, so one
market fetch prices every position in it. Re-check after every oracle push
(subscribe to the market account or the `IndexPushed` event) since index
moves, not time, are what push positions underwater.

## Executing a liquidation

Liquidation is cash-only: no synth to buy, no reserve to return tokens to. You
call `liquidate()` and are paid a SOL bonus.

```typescript
await program.methods
  .liquidate()
  .accountsPartial({
    market: marketPda,
    solVault: vaultPda,
    insurance: insurancePda,
    position: shortPda,            // ["short", market, victimOwner]
    positionOwner: victimOwner,    // receives any collateral remainder
    liquidator: wallet.publicKey,
  })
  .rpc();
```

What you receive, in lamports:

```
bonus  = notional_value * liq_bonus_bps / 10000
payout = min(bonus, collateral remaining after the loss is settled)
       + insurance top-up if that remainder is short of the bonus
```

The position's underwater loss is settled into the insurance fund, your bonus
is paid from collateral (topped up by the fund if needed), and any remainder is
refunded to the position owner. v1 liquidations are full: the entire position
closes in one call, there is no partial close.

## Economics

Your margin per liquidation is the SOL bonus (`liq_bonus_bps`, for example
5% of the position's index-valued notional) minus transaction fees (negligible
on Solana). Because liquidation is cash-only, there is **no synth to acquire**,
so no AMM slippage and no inventory or direction risk in sourcing tokens: the
profit is simply the bonus.

The one caveat is a deeply underwater position whose collateral cannot cover
the bonus: then you depend on the [insurance fund](/concepts/insurance-fund)
for the top-up, and an exhausted fund means a partial bonus. Watch the fund's
`insurance_lamports` and prefer markets that are seeded.

## Running the funding crank

`accrueFunding()` is permissionless and takes only the market account. It reads
the pushed mark, applies the clamped funding rate to the global funding index,
and rejects calls more frequent than `min_crank_interval_secs` with
`CrankTooSoon`.

```typescript
await program.methods
  .accrueFunding()
  .accountsPartial({ market: marketPda })
  .rpc();
```

Crank every market on a timer slightly above the minimum interval. There is no
direct payment for cranking in v1; keepers run it because stale funding delays
the PnL drift that creates liquidation opportunities, and because a live
funding index keeps position math (and therefore your monitoring) accurate.

<Note>
Liquidation and the crank both require the market to be `Live` and not frozen,
and both require a fresh index. During a
[circuit breaker](/concepts/circuit-breaker) freeze, positions cannot be
liquidated; expect a queue of eligible positions when the market unfreezes,
and price the reopening race accordingly.
</Note>
