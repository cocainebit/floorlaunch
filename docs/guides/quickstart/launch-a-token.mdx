---
title: "Launch a token"
description: "Create a market against any listed collectible in about a minute."
---

Every ***commas*** token is launched against a real collectible: a graded card or an NFT collection floor. You do not need to own the collectible to launch, and any collectible can have any number of tokens launched against it.

## What you need

- A Solana wallet ([Phantom](https://phantom.com), [Solflare](https://solflare.com), or an embedded wallet)
- About **0.15 SOL**: the 0.1 SOL launch fee plus transaction costs

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a market">
    Open **Launch** and choose the underlying. Collectibles are grouped by
    category: NFT collections, Pokemon, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic: The
    Gathering. The list shows each collectible's live floor and liquidity so
    you can judge how solid the underlying market is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a fee receiver">
    Half of the trading fees your market generates go to a receiver you
    pick. Send them to your own wallet, any address, or an identity: an X
    handle, a YouTube channel, or an [Elite Fourum](https://elitefourum.com) account. Identity fees
    accrue in an on-chain escrow that only the verified owner can claim (via
    X OAuth, YouTube, or Elite Fourum proof), so you can route fees to a
    community figure who has never heard of you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personalise">
    Ticker, name, image, and links. Tickers are prefixed `fl` and checked
    for availability as you type. The image is uploadable; links split into
    X, website, Discord, Telegram, and the collectible's marketplace page.
    The name and image become the token's [on-chain metadata](/concepts/synth-token#identity),
    so it renders as the collectible on Solscan and terminals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and deploy">
    The summary shows everything, including the launch fee. Deploying sends
    0.1 SOL to the protocol treasury and launches the token as a
    [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) Dynamic Bonding Curve pool. The fee
    payment is verified on-chain before the market goes live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Your token launches as a Meteora DBC pool with a **fixed 1B supply and mint authority revoked**, all of it the tradeable float. It trades through Jupiter from block one and is visible on Axiom, Photon, and Solscan. As the curve fills, Meteora migrates it to a DAMM v2 pool: no button, no waiting.

From then on you earn **half of the 0.70% fee on every trade** (and every item swap), paid out automatically.

<Note>
  The oracle starts tracking the collectible's live price at launch, so your
  market's premium always measures the token against the real thing.
</Note>
