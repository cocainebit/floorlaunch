title: "Sell a token"
description: "Exit to SOL on the Meteora pool, routed by Jupiter."
---

Selling mirrors buying: same venue, same fee, quoted before you sign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick an amount">
    The panel quotes the SOL you will receive after the 0.7% fee and shows
    the price impact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your sell routes through **Jupiter** into the token's
    [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) pool (a Dynamic Bonding Curve at launch,
    a DAMM v2 pool after migration). The fill lands in one transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exiting into the real thing

If the market's item reserve and escrow hold copies of the collectible, you have a second exit: instead of selling for SOL, swap your tokens for an actual copy. See [Take out the card](/guides/quickstart/redeem-a-card).

<Note>
  Selling is never gated: no lockups, no windows, no allowlists. The Meteora
  pool trades around the clock, and commas cannot pause it.
</Note>
