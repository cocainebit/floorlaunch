---
title: "Buy a token"
description: "Pay with SOL through Jupiter, or swap in a real copy of the collectible."
---

There are two ways in: SOL, like any token, or the collectible itself.

## Pay with SOL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the market and pick an amount">
    The trade panel quotes exactly what you will receive, the 0.7% fee,
    and the price impact before you sign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your buy routes through **Jupiter** into the token's [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag)
    pool (a Dynamic Bonding Curve at launch, a DAMM v2 pool after migration).
    Either way the fill lands in one transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pay with the collectible

If you hold a registered on-chain copy of the underlying (a vaulted card NFT or a collection NFT), you can swap it in instead of paying SOL.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to 'Pay with the collectible'">
    The panel detects registered copies in your wallet and shows the item's
    live value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap it in">
    You receive tokens **equal in value to the copy at the oracle value**,
    minus the 0.7% swap fee: not a fixed amount. If the token trades cheap,
    one copy buys more tokens; if it trades rich, fewer. The copy goes into the
    market's escrow, where anyone exiting can later swap it out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Item swaps price off a smoothed mark, so the rate cannot be manipulated
  inside a single transaction. They draw from a funded item reserve; if the
  reserve is empty, swapping a copy in is unavailable until withdrawals refill
  it.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **The item swap is optional redemption, not custody by default.** Buying the
  token gives you the collectible's price exposure; swapping the real copy in
  and out is the door for anyone who actually wants to move the physical item.
  It pays the same 0.7% fee a SOL trade does.
</Tip>
