Smart contract risk is real everywhere, and pretending otherwise is how users get hurt. This page lists the failure modes that have actually burned launchpad users, what ***commas*** does about each, and what risk remains.

## The classic launchpad failure modes

| Risk | What happens elsewhere | ***commas*** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hidden mint | Team mints supply after launch and dumps | **Impossible by construction**: the SPL mint is created with a fixed 1,000,000,000 supply and its mint authority is revoked at creation; nothing can ever mint again |
| Curve rug | Creator edits curve parameters mid-flight | The curve is [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag)'s Dynamic Bonding Curve, not commas code; neither the creator nor commas can edit the pool's schedule mid-flight |
| Fake liquidity | Pool seeded thin, drained by insiders | Liquidity lives in Meteora's audited DBC that migrates to a DAMM v2 pool; commas never holds the pool and cannot pull it |
| Oracle manipulation | A bad price feed liquidates or misprices everyone | Oracle pushes are EMA-smoothed and rate-limited; any push deviating hard from the trend **freezes the commas machinery** instead of moving it (circuit breaker), and stale feeds block the risky operations entirely |
| Sandwich pricing | Swap rates read spot price, attacker moves it in the same block | Item swaps and funding price off a **time-weighted mark**, which a single-transaction spike barely moves |
| Insolvent shorts | Bad debt socialized to everyone | Cash-settled shorts are overcollateralized (150% to open, 120% maintenance), settle in SOL against a per-market insurance fund, and total short open interest is capped by `max_open_interest` |

## The Meteora leg

Every launch runs on **Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve into DAMM v2**: contracts that are audited, immutable to us, and already carry billions in volume across the Solana ecosystem. commas never holds custody of the curve or the pool; we attach the synthetic layer (oracle index, cash-settled shorts, item swaps) alongside, and the 0.7% fee split is enforced by Meteora's own program, not ours.

This is a deliberate architecture choice: the highest-risk piece of any launchpad, the money-holding curve, runs on the most battle-tested contracts on Solana rather than on fresh code.

## What risk remains

- **The oracle key.** The index and mark are pushed by a signed relayer. A stolen key can move the index by at most `breaker_bps` per push before the market freezes, and the [funding clamp](/concepts/funding) bounds the damage of even accepted pushes, but the key is a trusted component. See [Circuit Breaker](/concepts/circuit-breaker).
- **Graded-card attestation.** Graded-card indexes are operator-attested from [Collector Crypt](https://collectorcrypt.com) floors today, not a fully automated feed. NFT floors read live from Magic Eden. Treat card indexes as operator-maintained until the automated pipeline ships (see [Roadmap](/roadmap)).
- **The insurance fund is bounded.** It is the short side's counterparty. If it is exhausted, closing shorts may not be paid full profit and liquidators may get a partial bonus until it is replenished with `fund_insurance`. See [Insurance Fund](/concepts/insurance-fund).
