---
title: "Hedging with Shorts"
description: "Lock in the value of a card or NFT you hold by opening a cash-settled short: post SOL collateral, settle PnL in SOL against the index."
---

If you hold the underlying (a graded card, an NFT from the collection), you
can hedge its downside without selling it: post SOL collateral and open a
**cash-settled short**. Your PnL settles in SOL against the oracle index, so
if the market falls, your short profit offsets the loss on the physical
asset. You keep the asset, its provenance, and any holder benefits.

Shorts are the natural counter-side of the whole system: holders of the
underlying are structurally motivated to be short it. See
[Shorts](/concepts/shorts) for the mechanics.

<Note>
This replaced the old draw-and-sell hedge. You no longer draw synth from a
reserve and sell it, and there is no `repay_burn` to close. You post
collateral, hold a notional short, and `close_position` settles your PnL in
SOL directly. No tokens are ever handled.
</Note>

## Opening a short

The market must be `Live` and not frozen. Your collateral must cover the
short's notional at the initial collateral ratio (`initial_cr_bps`, for
example 150%):

```
collateral >= notional_value * initial_cr / 100
notional_value = size_base_units * index_twap / 1e12
```

```typescript
// Short one unit (1,000,000 tokens of notional) against 150 SOL collateral
await program.methods
  .openShort(new BN(150 * LAMPORTS_PER_SOL), new BN("1000000000000"))
  .accountsPartial({
    market: marketPda,
    solVault: vaultPda,
    insurance: insurancePda,
    position: shortPda,       // ["short", market, owner]
    owner: wallet.publicKey,
  })
  .rpc();
```

No synth is drawn and nothing is sold. Your SOL collateral sits in the market
vault, and your position carries a one-unit short notional against the index.

## A worked example

Index: 100 SOL per unit. You hold a card worth about 100 SOL and want to hedge
all of it.

- Short one unit (1e12 base units). Notional value = 100 SOL.
- At 150% initial CR you must post at least 150 SOL. Say you post 150 SOL.
- Maintenance CR is 120%, so you are liquidatable when
  `150 / index < 1.2`, that is when the index rises above 125 SOL.

If the floor drops to 70 SOL: your card lost about 30 SOL of value, but your
short's PnL is `1e12 * (100 - 70) / 1e12 = 30 SOL` of profit, paid from the
[insurance fund](/concepts/insurance-fund) at close, and your collateral is
returned. The hedge worked.

If the floor rips to 130 SOL: your card gained 30 SOL, but your short is 30 SOL
underwater and past maintenance. Either you topped up collateral on the way up
and closed the 30 SOL loss out of collateral, or a
[liquidator](/concepts/liquidations) closed you out and the loss was settled
from your collateral. Hedges cap your upside; that is what a hedge is.

## Managing the position

| Action | Instruction | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add collateral | `addCollateral(amount)` | Always allowed, even frozen |
| Remove collateral | `withdrawCollateral(amount)` | Post-withdraw CR must stay at or above initial CR |
| Close | `closePosition()` | Settles PnL in SOL against the insurance fund, returns remaining collateral, refunds rent |

Watch your CR as the index moves. The index used everywhere is the smoothed
`index_twap`, not raw pushes, so you have some time to react to spikes, but
not unlimited time.

## Funding while short

Funding is folded into your settleable PnL, so [funding](/concepts/funding)
applies continuously without you touching the position:

- **Token above index** (positive premium): funding pays shorts. Your PnL
  drifts up slowly. You are being paid to hold the tether side.
- **Token below index** (negative premium): funding charges shorts. Your PnL
  drifts down slowly, nudging you to close, which is exactly the pressure that
  lifts the price back toward the index.

Rates are clamped per day (`max_funding_bps_per_day`), so funding is a drift,
never a shock.

## Closing out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your PnL">
    Read your position and the market. Your settleable PnL is
    `size * (index_entry - index_twap) / 1e12` plus accrued funding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close">
    `closePosition()` settles the PnL in SOL against the insurance fund: profit
    is paid to you and your collateral is returned; a loss is deducted from
    your collateral and paid into the fund, and you keep the remainder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Liquidation is real: if the index rises enough that your CR falls below
maintenance, anyone can liquidate you, and the liquidator's bonus comes out of
your collateral. Size the hedge and your buffer so a normal rally does not
liquidate you. See [Liquidations](/concepts/liquidations).
</Warning>
