title: "Going Long"
description: "Buy the token on its Meteora pool through Jupiter to get liquid exposure to a collectible market."

Going long on ***commas*** means holding a market's token. It is a
funding-pegged synthetic: its pool price is pulled toward the oracle index by
funding and cash-settled shorts, not locked to it. You are buying exposure to
the underlying's price in SOL terms, with a soft peg.

<Note>
Expect a basis of a few percent between the token price and the index. The
[funding mechanism](/concepts/funding) narrows persistent gaps over time; it
does not eliminate them block by block.
</Note>

## Where you trade

Every market's token trades on a [Meteora](https://www.meteora.ag) pool: a
Dynamic Bonding Curve at launch that migrates to a DAMM v2 pool. You buy and
sell it the same way you trade any Solana token, routed by **Jupiter**, whether
from the commas app, a Jupiter interface, or any aggregator. commas does not
run its own curve or AMM, so there is no separate bootstrap-versus-live venue
to think about: it is one Jupiter-routed pool the whole way.

The pool charges a **0.7%** base fee, split 50/50 creator/protocol.

## Buying and selling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the market">
    The token is a standard SPL mint whose metadata brands it as the
    collectible, so it appears on Axiom, Photon, Solscan, and Jupiter. Grab the
    mint from the market page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote and set slippage">
    Quote the swap through Jupiter, which routes into the Meteora pool and
    shows the 0.7% fee and price impact. Set the slippage you will accept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap">
    Confirm the Jupiter swap. Buying SOL to the token gives you long exposure;
    selling the token back to SOL exits it. No lockups, no gates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reading premium and basis

Two fields on the `Market` account tell you where the token trades relative to
the underlying:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `index_twap` | Oracle index, lamports per whole underlying unit |
| `mark_ema` | Smoothed Meteora pool price, lamports per whole underlying unit |

The premium is `(mark_ema - index_twap) / index_twap`. A positive premium
means the token trades rich to the index; [funding](/concepts/funding) is then
paying shorts to lean against it, and hedgers open cash-settled shorts at the
rich mark. A negative premium means the token trades cheap; shorts are being
charged, and the long side collects that funding.

Remember the scale: 1,000,000 tokens represent one whole underlying unit (one
floor NFT or one graded card), and the token has 6 decimals.

<Warning>
The commas-side machinery (shorts, funding, item swaps, oracle pushes) can be
frozen by the [circuit breaker](/concepts/circuit-breaker), but the Meteora
pool itself keeps trading: commas does not custody the pool. Your balances and
positions are untouched by a freeze; only the synthetic layer pauses.
</Warning>
